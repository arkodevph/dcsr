# Google Flow hero loop

Use the [hero poster](public/assets/hero-aircon-poster-v2.webp) as a visual reference. In Flow, choose a landscape video and an available 6–8 second length. If the selected model offers first and last frames, use the same poster for both and review the seam before exporting. [Google's Flow help](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en) documents frame inputs; [supported lengths depend on the model](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en).

## Copy-ready prompt

> Static wide shot of one unbranded white air-conditioning condenser on a clean white platform beneath a pale blue sky. Its fan turns gently while a few thin, translucent cyan airflow ribbons move in a slow, smooth repeating wave across the lower half of the frame. Keep the unit centered in the lower third and leave the upper half clear for website text. Soft daylight, photorealistic, minimal blue-and-white palette. Locked camera; no zoom, pan, cuts, lighting changes, people, grass, hills, logos, text, particles, or sound. End with the fan and airflow in the same visual position as the first frame for a seamless loop.

Keep the final export short and muted in the website. The page places its headline and buttons in React above the video; do not generate text into the clip.
